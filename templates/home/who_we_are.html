Our Story

Jeevitha P Raju

Founder - Leadership Innovation World

Soft Skills and Communication Coach | Transforming 500,000+ Professionals | Personal Growth Strategist

Jeevitha P Raju, with an MBA from St. Joseph's Institute of Management, is a leading soft skills and communication coach who blends corporate training with the arts. She has empowered over 500,000 professionals, including top industry leaders, using her innovative approach to enhance leadership qualities. Known for her engaging keynote talks and extensive workshops, Jeevitha aims to create standout leaders by focusing on impactful communication and personal growth.

Rohit Rammurthy

Director - Leadership Innovation World

Deputy Director | PES University, India

Rohith Ramurthy, Deputy Director at PES University, combines a global education background with dynamic leadership expertise. With a Master’s from Indiana University of Pennsylvania and experience at prestigious forums, including the Harvard Project for Asian & International Relations, Rohith integrates his international insights into innovative teaching. Passionate about leadership, he has curated impactful webinars and workshops, reaching over 2 million participants and featuring industry leaders like Dr. Philip Kotler.
